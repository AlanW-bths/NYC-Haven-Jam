# NYC-Haven-Jam: Wheel it Over
Our game "Wheel it Over" is a story of a mouse, trying to bring the impossibly bouncy wheel of cheese back to its' home.
The controls are arrow keys for movement, space to jump, and shift to sprint. R acts as a reset button.
Jumping is the key to success.

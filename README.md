# NYC-Haven-Jam
Our game "Wheel it Over" is a story of a mouse, trying to bring the impossibly bouncy wheel of cheese back to its' home. The player will have great difficulty with the wheel, as it will keep bouncing back to the beginning. The player will eventually have to bounce back from the impossible dream of that cheese, and return to its' home without it.
The controls are arrow keys for movement, space to jump, and shift to sprint. R acts as a reset button.
